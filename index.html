About

I'm a Process Engineering Manager at SpaceX, working on providing high-speed, low-latency internet to the world with Starlink. I graduated from MIT in 2018 with a Bachelor's degree in Materials Science and Engineering.

Since then I've been involved in a wide range of projects: reliability testing the solar arrays and battery packs on the Dragon spacecraft; formulating custom blends of injection-molding plastics, printed electronics and inks, and radome coatings and materials for high-volume consumer electronics; and, over the last several years, creating and leading a team to bring up greenfield manufacturing of printed circuit boards (now the largest PCB manufacturer in North America) and solar cells (soon to be the largest in North America as well) for Starlink — from initial conception, buyoff, and capital procurement (>$1B), through process design, layout, facilities and construction design, install and commissioning, engineering and production org hiring, and process bring-up to steady state and handover.

I specialize in process engineering and in bringing extremely complex manufacturing processes up from scratch, cradle to grave.

At MIT I worked on a variety of materials science research and prototyping projects, and I've kept building since: I've made my own electron microscope that can "see" atoms, injection-molded custom yo-yos, designed and built a carbon fiber bike, and built a handful of 3D printers and CNC machines. I always have a few things in the works.

Outside of work I'm an avid backpacker, camper, hiker, rock climber, canoer, rower, cook, baker, and entrepreneur. Lately I've been doing more woodworking and stained glass.

Internships

While at Apple, I worked primarily on the AirPower wireless charging mat and the next-generation iPhone 8 and 8 Plus. I designed and fabricated several test setups in collaboration with electrical engineering, materials, and product design teams for benchmarking technologies and conducting user experience studies. I extensively used NX CAD, ANSYS mechanical, thermal, and electromagnetic simulation software, and MATLAB.

At CEiiA, I performed market analysis and research on battery management system (BMS) options for a large 30 kg UAV. I designed multiple BMS options in Eagle and had them manufactured and tested. I also performed several carbon fiber layups for another intern's 3-axis gimbal design.
