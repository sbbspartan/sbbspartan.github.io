Teams and research

Activities

I was lucky enough to be involved in several clubs and labs while at MIT, including the MIT Energy Club, the MIT Electric Vehicle Team, and the MIT FSAE team. I loved my time on these teams, and they taught me a heck of a lot about practical engineering.

Team

MIT Electric FSAE

Part of the MIT FSAE team for over a year, where we built an electric racecar. I worked primarily on the carbon fiber fairings — design work and custom layups.
Team

MIT Electric Vehicle Team

Over a year on the MIT EVT, working on an electric racing motorcycle for the Pikes Peak hill climb — we placed 2nd in our category. I worked on fairing design and layups and integrated a number of sensors into the bike.
Research

MIT Electrochemical Energy Lab (EEL)

Research into catalysis and lithium-ion battery technology over the summer of my freshman year.
Research

MIT Grossman Group

During my freshman and sophomore years I worked in the Grossman group measuring the thermal conductivity of some interesting graphitic cements and developing a thermal conductivity metering device.
