Novel Casting Sand Formulation

In a team of four, over the course of one semester, we developed a novel inorganic casting sand formulation that is both strong, reusable, produces high quality castings, and is non-toxic. This sand formulation has already found some use within the MIT foundary, and is undergoing more testing.
